Skip bins in Geurie

Geurie is a village on the Mitchell Highway, about 28 km from Dubbo by road. Here's when a skip makes more sense than a tip run, and how to get a free quote.

Last checked 24 October 2026

Skip hire in Geurie

Geurie sits between Dubbo and Wellington, with farmland all around. Jobs here are often shed and yard clean-ups, old fencing and roofing iron, and house clear-outs. A 6–9 m³ skip suits most shed and farm jobs, and 3–4 m³ a garage or single room.

Why a skip instead of the local tip

Dubbo Regional Council's Geurie Transfer Station at 214 Comobella Road is open Sunday 10am to 3:50pm and Thursday 1pm to 3:50pm, for local residents without a kerbside bin service. Council says loads over 1 cubic metre aren't accepted at any rural transfer station. For anything bigger than a trailer load, a skip or a trip to Whylandra or Wellington is the practical option.

Access

Tell the operator about gravel tracks, gates and soft red soil after rain. A firm spot near the shed or house is best.

Council rules

On your own land a skip generally doesn't need approval. On the road, Dubbo Regional Council requires section 68 approval with fees. See the permit guide.
